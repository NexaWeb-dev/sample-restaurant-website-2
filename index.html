Our Story

A table set for unhurried hours.

Aurelia began as a twelve-seat coffee counter and grew, patiently, into a restaurant built around one idea: that good hospitality is a form of attention.

Philosophy

We cook with the seasons and say no to shortcuts. Menus change as the markets do, and every plate is finished to order.

Ingredients

Single-estate coffee, stone-milled flour, farm-fresh produce and dairy from small producers we know by name.

Hospitality

Our team is trained to anticipate rather than interrupt — so you can simply enjoy the room, the food and the company.

The Atmosphere

Light, linen & low conversation.

Hand-plastered walls, walnut tables and brass pendants that glow rather than glare. By day the room fills with soft window light; by evening it settles into candlelit calm.
